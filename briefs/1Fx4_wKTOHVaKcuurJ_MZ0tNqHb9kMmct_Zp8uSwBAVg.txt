Key Information: (summary of the film for context)
The Man With the Silver Case is a black and white Hitchcockian thriller from writer and director Colin Best: a courier handcuffed to a mysterious silver case has to deliver it across a city that wants what is inside, and a series of payphone calls keeps pulling him deeper into the job. It is on Prime Video and free on Kapang. DSO is pulling the story into the real world with the Silver Case Manhunt, a $25,000 Los Angeles hunt built around the film, its clues and a community solving them together. The finale structure is seven silver cases, one real and six decoys, exclusive to the October 27 to 29 finale, with around 100 verified finalists and the winner announced October 30. There are no cans hidden around LA and no secondary prizes to advertise. The participant journey is lookagain.la: watch the film (the site says the first clue is already in it), follow @SilverCaseOfficial, and register when registration and the Official Rules open on October 2. Watching the film or visiting the site does not by itself qualify anyone for the finale. Angles to lean into: the "wait, this movie has a real $25K manhunt attached to it" hook; the case itself and never knowing what is inside; the courier's paranoia and the tense chase and confrontation beats; the black and white look next to modern LA; character edits and "something you missed" details; and once the cleared hunt footage lands, the coffee cart, the stunt and the creators making the hunt tangible on the streets of LA. Clips should feel like a mystery you can actually step into, not an ad. Every post points to watching the film and lookagain.la
